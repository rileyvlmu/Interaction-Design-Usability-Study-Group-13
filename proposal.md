
# Usability Study Proposal: Duolingo vs. LingoDeer

## Study Objective

For this study, I want to compare Duolingo and LingoDeer to see which app is easier to use for someone learning Japanese. Both apps help people learn languages, but they organize their lessons and activities differently. I'm interested in finding out which app makes it easier for new users to find lessons, practice vocabulary, and learn useful phrases.

## Systems Being Compared

**Duolingo:** Duolingo uses short lessons, progress tracking, and game-like features to make learning a language feel more interactive.

**LingoDeer:** LingoDeer focuses on structured lessons, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation.

Since both apps offer Japanese lessons, I think they would be a good pair to compare. They have a similar purpose but different approaches to teaching and organizing their content.

## Three Proposed Tasks

I plan to have participants complete the same three tasks in both apps.

1. **Start a beginner Japanese lesson:** Starting from the main screen, find and start a beginner Japanese lesson and answer the first exercise.
2. **Find a useful Japanese phrase:** Find the Japanese phrase for "Thank you" and play the audio to hear how it is pronounced.
3. **Complete a vocabulary activity:** Find a Japanese vocabulary practice or review activity and answer five questions.

These tasks represent things someone might want to do when starting to learn Japanese, especially if they want to use the language while traveling. Before the study begins, I will check that all three tasks can be completed in both apps using their available features.

## Usability Metrics

I plan to measure learnability, errors, and satisfaction.

### Learnability

I will time how long it takes participants to complete each task. Participants will not receive instructions on how to use either app beforehand, so I can see how easily they figure things out on their own. They should be unfamiliar with the apps but understand what each task is asking them to do.

### Errors

I will keep track of times when participants make an unexpected choice or end up somewhere they did not intend to go. For example, opening the wrong section or selecting the wrong activity would count as an error. App crashes and software bugs will not count because I want to focus on how people interact with the interface.

### Satisfaction

After each task, participants will rate how much they enjoyed the experience on a scale from 1 to 10. This will help me understand which app people prefer using, in addition to how quickly and successfully they complete the tasks.

### Why I Am Omitting Efficiency

I am leaving out efficiency because it is better measured with people who already know how to use an app or have had time to practice. I want to focus on the experience of people who are unfamiliar with the apps, so learnability makes more sense for this study.

The other usability metric commonly discussed alongside these measures is memorability. It is not one of the four options listed for this assignment's metric selection. Measuring it would also require testing whether people remember how to use an app after some time has passed.

## How I Plan to Conduct the Study

I plan to ask participants to complete all three tasks in both apps. I will record how long each task takes, note any errors, and ask participants to rate their experience afterward. I will try to recruit at least eight participants, depending on availability.

To make the comparison fair, everyone will receive the same task instructions. When possible, I will also vary which app participants use first so that the order does not unfairly favor one app.

## How I Will Compare the Results

I will prioritize learnability because I want to find out which app is easier for beginners to figure out. Errors will be my second priority because confusing interfaces can cause users to make mistakes or struggle to find what they need. Satisfaction will be my third priority because an app should also be enjoyable to use.

After collecting the results, I will compare the two apps across all three metrics and decide which one provides the better overall experience. I will also consider situations where one app performs better in one area but worse in another.
